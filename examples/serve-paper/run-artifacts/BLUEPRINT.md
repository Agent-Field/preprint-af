## Venue notes

High‑impact ML systems venue (e.g., MLSys, NeurIPS, ICML). Emphasis on a novel architectural contribution (pluggable cache gate), rigorous empirical validation, and practical efficiency. The paper should be concise, with clear figures and tables, and a strong narrative around margin‑aware reuse decisions. The related work should be framed to highlight the gap, and the method section must be crisp and reproducible. The analysis section is crucial for a systems paper, showing not just raw numbers but when and why the method works.
